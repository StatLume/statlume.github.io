Before you start

What StatLume needs.

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer for the current public release.
  • A Google account with access to a YouTube channel and YouTube Studio.
  • An internet connection for Studio authentication and realtime updates.

First connection

Choose Connect YouTube account, sign in inside the embedded browser, and select the correct channel. StatLume opens the realtime analytics view automatically.
