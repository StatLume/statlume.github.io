Features

YouTube Studio numbers, without the browser clutter.

StatLume keeps the near-real-time channel data you check most often in a lightweight desktop widget built for focus.

Explore realtime stats

At a glance

Everything useful stays close.

Choose the amount of information that fits the moment. StatLume can show the complete channel view, shrink to a compact live count, or fill a second screen.

The embedded YouTube Studio session and recent snapshot remain on your device. If Studio is temporarily unavailable, the last valid data stays visible while recovery attempts continue at controlled intervals.

Three time periods

Move between the latest minute, 60 minutes, and 48 hours without changing your workspace.

Flexible display

Use Normal, Compact, or Fullscreen view, adjust opacity, and keep the widget above other windows.

Local by design

Your Studio browser session, presentation data, and cached statistics stay on your computer.
