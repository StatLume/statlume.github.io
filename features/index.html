Features

YouTube Studio numbers, without the browser clutter.

StatLume keeps the realtime channel data you check most often in a lightweight desktop widget built for focus.

Download for Windows →Explore realtime stats

At a glance

Built around the way creators check performance.

Three realtime periods

Switch between the latest minute, 60 minutes, and 48 hours.

Per-video breakdown

See which recent videos contribute to the selected realtime period.

Normal and compact views

Keep the full chart visible or reduce the widget to the essential count.

Fullscreen presentation

Turn the realtime view into a clean display for a studio or second monitor.

Automatic recovery

Saved data remains visible while Studio is unavailable, with rate-limited retries.

Local session storage

Your embedded YouTube Studio browser session and cached statistics stay on your device.

Display

Fits the workspace you already use.

Choose the amount of information and screen space that makes sense right now.

Move between views instantly

Use the widget menu to choose Normal, Compact, or Fullscreen. Double-click Normal view to open Fullscreen, or double-click Compact view to return to Normal.

Keep it where it helps

Adjust opacity, keep the widget always on top, hide the video list, show a Windows taskbar counter, or start StatLume with Windows.

Privacy

Designed for local use.

StatLume reads responses produced by the authenticated YouTube Studio page inside its embedded browser. It does not send your channel statistics, Google password, authentication cookies, or browser session to a StatLume analytics backend.

Read the full Privacy Policy for details about local data, disconnection, and support submissions.
