Features

StatLume features for realtime YouTube analytics.

StatLume keeps the near-real-time channel data you check most often in a lightweight desktop widget built for focus.

Explore realtime stats

At a glance

Everything useful stays close.

Choose the amount of information that fits the moment. StatLume can show the complete channel view, shrink to a compact live count, or fill a second screen.

The embedded YouTube Studio session and recent snapshot remain on your device. If Studio is temporarily unavailable, the last valid data stays visible while recovery attempts continue at controlled intervals.

Three realtime periods

Switch between the latest minute, 60 minutes, and 48 hours.

Per-video breakdown

See which recent videos contribute to the selected realtime period.

Normal and compact views

Keep the full chart visible or reduce the widget to the essential count.

Fullscreen presentation

Turn the realtime view into a clean display for a studio or second monitor.

Automatic recovery

Saved data remains visible while Studio is unavailable, with rate-limited retries.

Local session storage

Your embedded Studio session and cached statistics stay on your device.

Display

Fits the workspace you already use.

Move between Normal, Compact, and Fullscreen views at any time. Adjust opacity, keep the widget always on top, hide the video list, show a Windows taskbar counter, or start StatLume with Windows.

StatLume reads responses produced by the authenticated YouTube Studio page locally. It does not send your channel statistics, Google password, authentication cookies, or browser session to a StatLume analytics backend.
