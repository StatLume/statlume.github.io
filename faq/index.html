Common questions

The essentials, in plain language.

Google handles authentication inside the embedded browser. StatLume does not receive or store your Google password.

Your session, cached presentation data, preferences, and recent snapshot remain on your computer during normal use.

How does StatLume connect?

Choose Connect YouTube account and sign in inside the embedded Chromium browser.

Which periods are available?

Select the latest minute, 60 minutes, or 48 hours in Settings.

Why can numbers differ?

Different Studio reports may use different ranges, processing delays, or definitions.

What happens offline?

The status changes to OFF, the last valid snapshot remains visible, and retries use increasing delays.

Where is data stored?

Recent presentation data, preferences, and the embedded browser profile remain locally on your device.

Is StatLume made by Google?

No. StatLume is independent and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.

Display and startup

Designed to stay out of the way.

Use Normal, Compact, or Fullscreen view. You can adjust opacity, hide the video list, keep the widget always on top, and enable a taskbar counter.

StatLume can launch with Windows. If you need more help, send the details through the Support page.
