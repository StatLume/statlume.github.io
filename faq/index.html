Setup and accounts

Getting connected.

How does StatLume connect to YouTube Studio? +

Choose Connect YouTube account and sign in inside the embedded Chromium browser. Google and YouTube handle authentication within that browser session. StatLume then opens the realtime analytics page automatically.

Does StatLume receive my Google password? +

No. Your password is entered into the Google sign-in page inside the embedded browser. StatLume does not receive or store it.

How do I switch YouTube accounts or channels? +

Choose Disconnect account, connect again, and sign in with the account and channel you want to use. Disconnecting clears cached channel data and schedules the local Studio browser profile for deletion.

Can StatLume start with Windows? +

Yes. Enable Start with Windows in Settings. Microsoft Store installations use Windows Startup Apps; portable installations use the current user’s startup configuration.

Statistics and views

Reading the widget.

Which realtime periods are available? +

You can select the latest minute, 60 minutes, or 48 hours in Settings. The total, chart, and video breakdown adapt to the chosen period when that information is available from Studio.

What is the difference between Normal, Compact, and Fullscreen? +

Normal shows the full dashboard. Compact reduces the widget to the essential count. Fullscreen presents the statistics cleanly across a display. Use the widget menu or double-click to move between views.

What does OFF mean? +

OFF means fresh Studio data is temporarily unavailable. StatLume keeps the last valid snapshot visible and retries with increasing delays. It returns to LIVE when new data arrives.

Why might the displayed numbers differ from another YouTube page? +

StatLume displays the realtime information available from the signed-in YouTube Studio page. Different Studio reports can use different time ranges, processing delays, or definitions, so their numbers may not match exactly.

Privacy and availability

Where your data stays.

Does StatLume upload my channel analytics? +

Not during normal application use. Realtime responses are processed locally, and the recent snapshot, channel presentation data, and preferences remain on your device.

Why can YouTube Studio changes affect StatLume? +

StatLume depends on the authenticated Studio page and the responses it produces. Google or YouTube can change those interfaces or temporarily make them unavailable.

Is StatLume made by YouTube or Google? +

No. StatLume is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
