Common questions

The essentials, in plain language.

Google handles authentication inside the embedded browser. StatLume does not receive or store your Google password.

Your session, cached presentation data, preferences, and recent snapshot remain on your computer during normal use.

Which periods are available?

The latest minute, 60 minutes, and 48 hours. The chart and video breakdown adapt when that information is available from Studio.

What happens offline?

The status changes to OFF, the last valid snapshot remains visible, and recovery attempts use increasing delays.

Is StatLume made by Google?

No. StatLume is an independent application and is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
