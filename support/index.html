StatLume Support

Send a support request without leaving the StatLume website. Do not include passwords, cookies, session tokens, or other account credentials.

Common issues

StatLume shows Offline. Open YouTube Studio through StatLume and verify that the account is still signed in.

Realtime statistics stop updating. Restart StatLume and reconnect the Studio session. YouTube Studio changes can temporarily affect StatLume.

Changing YouTube accounts. Use Disconnect in StatLume, then connect again with the desired account.
