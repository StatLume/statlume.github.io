Quick answers

Common issues

StatLume shows “Offline” +

Open YouTube Studio through StatLume and make sure you are still signed in.

Realtime statistics stop updating +

Restart StatLume and reconnect your YouTube Studio session. Changes to YouTube Studio may temporarily affect StatLume.

Changing YouTube accounts +

Use Disconnect in StatLume, then connect again with the account you want to use.
