Support

Send a support request directly from the StatLume website.

Do not include passwords, authentication cookies, session tokens, API keys, or other account credentials.

Common issues

StatLume shows “Offline”Open YouTube Studio through StatLume and make sure you are still signed in.
Realtime statistics stop updatingRestart StatLume and reconnect your YouTube Studio session. Changes to YouTube Studio may temporarily affect StatLume.
Changing YouTube accountsUse Disconnect in StatLume, then connect again with the desired account.
