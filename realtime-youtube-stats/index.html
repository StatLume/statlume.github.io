Realtime YouTube stats

See what your channel is doing right now.

StatLume brings the authenticated YouTube Studio view to your Windows desktop. The numbers are near-real-time and update as new data becomes available from Studio.

Time periods

Move from a spike to the bigger picture.

Latest minute

Use the minute view for the most immediate available activity. It is useful when a new upload, premiere, or external mention begins sending viewers to the channel.

Last 60 minutes

See the recent pattern as a chart and total. This is the default balance between immediacy and enough context to understand whether activity is sustained.

Last 48 hours

Switch to the broader YouTube Studio realtime period to compare the latest activity with the previous two days.

Continuity

Your last valid snapshot stays visible.

If YouTube Studio becomes temporarily unavailable, StatLume shows OFF and keeps the last valid local snapshot on screen. Recovery attempts use increasing delays rather than continuously reloading Studio.

When fresh data returns, the status switches back to LIVE and the retry schedule resets.

How it works

Studio remains the source.

You sign in through the embedded Chromium browser. StatLume processes the realtime responses produced by that authenticated Studio page locally and presents the useful fields in the desktop widget.

StatLume is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube. Studio changes can affect availability.
